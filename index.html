FAQ

Common questions

Do I need prior RPA or AI experience?

Module A assumes basic familiarity with business processes; we start from first principles on the tooling. Module B builds on concepts from Module A but can be taken standalone if you already automate.

Which platforms do you cover?

Concepts are platform-agnostic with hands-on labs using industry-standard tools. You'll receive guidance on mapping exercises to UiPath, Microsoft, and open-source stacks.
